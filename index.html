Why Beyond the Plaza

Built for the way travellers actually learn

Most Spanish courses teach you to fill in blanks. We teach you to hold a conversation at a street market, argue affectionately with a taxi driver, and read the room.

Conversation-first
Every module opens with a real-world hook — a situation you will actually be in. Grammar follows meaning, never the other way around.
Culture built in
Language without culture is a dictionary with no soul. Every module includes notes that tell you what your words actually mean to the people hearing them.
Structured for busy lives
Each module is self-contained and designed to fit in under 30 minutes. A1 through C1 — pick up exactly where your level sits.
