The hook
"The subjunctive isn't one tense — it's a lens you put over reality."
Core concepts
Subjuntivo presente: que corra · que hable · que tenga
Subjuntivo imperfecto: si corriera · si tuviera
Pluscuamperfecto: hubiera corrido · estuve vs. estaba
Key vocabulary
Que corra (present subj.) Si corriera (imperfect subj.) Hubiera corrido Si + imperfecto + condicional Estuve vs. estaba
Si yo corriera en un maratón, tendría mejores zapatos de correr.
If I ran in a marathon, I would have better running shoes.
Practice drill
Write 3 si-clauses: one dream / one regret / one open possibility.
Culture note — Estuve corriendo = completed. Estaba corriendo = interrupted by something. Tiny difference, essential in Spanish.
