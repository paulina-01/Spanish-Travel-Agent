The hook
"Spanish has two verbs for 'to be' — and choosing wrong makes you sound completely different."
Core concepts
Ser = permanent identity (who you are)
Estar = temporary state (how you feel)
Full pronoun conjugation for both verbs
Key vocabulary
soy / eres / es estoy / estás / está somos / estamos elegante · inteligente cansado · feliz
Nosotros somos carismáticos pero no somos elegantes.
We are charismatic but we are not elegant.
Practice drill
Ser or Estar? Complete: Yo ___ de Canadá. / Ella ___ cansada hoy.
Culture note — Estoy cansado means you had a long day — not that you're a tired person. Your mood is always temporary in Spanish.

Your conjugation base
"Every verb you'll ever learn lives on the same five tenses. Master the pattern once — with ser and estar — and every verb after follows."
The five indicative tenses
Presentnow
What is, or is happening, right now.
Soy ingeniero. Estoy cansado. — I am an engineer. I am tired.
Preteritecompleted past
A finished action — it happened, it's done.
Fui jugador de básquetbol, pero ya no juego. — I was a basketball player, but I don't play anymore.
Imperfectused-to · ongoing past
A background, habit, or "used to" in the past.
Yo estaba feliz el verano pasado. — I was happy last summer.
Conditionalpreview · would
What would be, under some condition.
Yo sería bombero de México si viviera ahí. — I would be a firefighter in Mexico if I lived there.
Futurepreview · will
What will be, later on.
Seré ingeniero. — I will be an engineer.
For A1 we drill Present · Preterite · Imperfect. Conditional & Future ride along as preview — you'll meet them fully later.

"Keep the word. Swap ser for estar — and you go from who you are to how you are right now."

The verb loop · ir
"One verb — ir — plus a plus any infinitive, and you can talk about the future. Including who you're going to ser or estar."

Fully irregular in the present — but the most-used verb in Spanish. Learn it first.
The near-future formula
voy / vas / va… + a + infinitivo = going to
a + el = al  ·  voy al restaurante
…including going to ser / estar
→ ser
Voy a ser ingeniero. — I'm going to be an engineer.
→ estar
Mañana voy a estar en Canadá. — Tomorrow I'm going to be in Canada.
→ ir
Vas a ir a la biblioteca. — You're going to go to the library.
question
¿A dónde vas a ir a comer? — Where are you going to eat?

Ayer yo fui jugador. (preterite)
fui · past
Mañana yo voy a estar en Canadá.
voy a estar · near future
Why?
Identity → ser. A state or place that could change → estar. A finished past → preterite (fui). A plan → voy a + infinitive.
