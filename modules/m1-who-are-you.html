The hook
"Spanish has two verbs for 'to be' — and choosing wrong makes you sound completely different."
Core concepts
Ser = permanent identity (who you are)
Estar = temporary state (how you feel)
Full pronoun conjugation for both verbs
Key vocabulary
soy / eres / es estoy / estás / está somos / estamos elegante · inteligente cansado · feliz
Nosotros somos carismáticos pero no somos elegantes.
We are charismatic but we are not elegant.
Practice drill
Ser or Estar? Complete: Yo ___ de Canadá. / Ella ___ cansada hoy.
Culture note — Estoy cansado means you had a long day — not that you're a tired person. Your mood is always temporary in Spanish.
